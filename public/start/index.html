A free app for the new West Virginia Medicaid rules

New Medicaid rules start in 2027. If you are on Medicaid and age 19 to 64, you may need to show that you work, volunteer, or go to school. If you can't show it, you could lose your coverage.

Hours Log helps you keep a record, so you are ready when the state asks.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • No sign-up
  • Your records stay on your phone

Are you on Medicaid?

You may get Medicaid and not know it. Look at your health insurance card. Many people with a card from one of these plans get their coverage through Medicaid:

  • Aetna Better Health of West Virginia
  • Highmark Health Options
  • The Health Plan
  • Wellpoint

If you are not sure, call the state at 1-877-716-1212 or check your account at wvpath.wv.gov.

What do I have to show?

Each month, you need one of these:

  • You earned at least $580 from work, or
  • You did at least 80 hours of work, volunteer work, job training, or school. You can add them together. For example, 50 hours of work and 30 hours of volunteer work make 80.

The state checks its own records first. If you work for yourself, do gig work, or get paid in cash, the state may have no record of your work. Then it is up to you to prove it.

Your household also has to stay under the Medicaid income limit. This is about how much your whole household can earn each month:

1 personabout $1,836
2 peopleabout $2,489
3 peopleabout $3,142
4 peopleabout $3,795
5 peopleabout $4,449
6 peopleabout $5,102

These are our estimates from the 2026 federal poverty numbers. The state decides the real limit.

Do I have to do this?

Some people do not. For example, you may not have to if you:

  • are pregnant, or were in the last 12 months
  • are a parent or caregiver of a child age 13 or younger, or care for a person with a disability
  • have a disability or a serious health problem
  • are in a drug or alcohol treatment program
  • are a veteran with a 100% disability rating
  • have Medicare

There are more. Use the state's checker at wvmedicaidhelp.org to see if the rules apply to you.

What does the app do?

  • Log your hours with a timer or by hand.
  • Log your income each month.
  • See each month at a glance: did you reach 80 hours or $580?
  • Count down to your Medicaid renewal date.
  • Make a report you can print or send to the state.
  • Save a backup, so you don't lose your records if you get a new phone.

It is free. No tricks. No ads. No credit card. No sign-up. Your records stay on your phone. We never see them.

How do I put it on my phone?

Hours Log is not in the app store. You get it from this website, and it works like any other app.

Android phone (Chrome):

  1. Tap Open Hours Log below.
  2. Tap I understand.
  3. Tap the Install button at the top of the screen. If you don't see it, tap ⋮ (top right), then Add to Home screen or Install app.

iPhone (Safari):

  1. Tap Open Hours Log below.
  2. Tap I understand.
  3. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Open Hours Log from the new icon on your home screen before you add any records.

After that, it works without internet.
