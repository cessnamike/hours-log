Hours Log for WV Medicaid (unofficial)

A poster to put up

Help people on Medicaid get ready for the new work rules. Print this poster and put it up at a library, office, church, clinic or agency. It is free to print, copy and post.

Download the poster to print (PDF)

One page, regular 8½ × 11 letter paper. Black and white is fine.

Download it as a picture

For Facebook, texts and email. Put the link hours.mikesheridandigital.com/start in your post too, because people can't scan a code on their own phone screen.

Good to know

  • The code on the poster opens hours.mikesheridandigital.com/start. People can also type that address.
  • For people without a smartphone, print our paper monthly log and keep copies next to the poster.
  • Hours Log is not from the State of West Virginia, the Department of Human Services or any Medicaid plan.
