Hours Log for the WV Medicaid Work Rule (unofficial) · Terms version 2026-11-18 · Rules last checked November 18, 2026

In plain words

  • Hours Log is a free tool for keeping your own record of hours and income.
  • It is not from the State of West Virginia, the WV Department of Human Services, any Medicaid plan, or any government agency.
  • It is not legal advice. The author is not a lawyer.
  • Only the state decides whether you meet the Medicaid work rule or qualify for an exemption.
  • Your records stay on your device. We never see them.

He is not a lawyer and does not represent the State of West Virginia, any state or federal agency, any Medicaid managed care plan, or anyone else. Nothing in the app or on this site is legal advice, and using it does not create any attorney-client or advisory relationship.

For advice about your own situation, contact the WV Department of Human Services at 1-877-716-1212, your Medicaid plan, or a legal aid organization.

2. The rules can change

Federal and state rules, and how the state applies them, can change at any time without notice to us. The app may not reflect the current rules. The date the rules were last checked is shown in the app and at the top of this page. Check the state's own information for yourself.

3. Use it as a record, not as a decision

You are welcome to use Hours Log to keep records. Do not rely on it to tell you whether you meet the rule. Messages like "month met" are only the app's own count of what you entered. They are not a decision by anyone. You are responsible for what you enter, for keeping your own proof (like pay stubs, tax records and signed volunteer sheets), for meeting deadlines, and for doing your own research or getting advice.

4. Your records and backups

Your records are saved only on your device. If your device is lost, broken, reset, or its browser data is cleared, your records can be lost. The app reminds you to save backups. Keeping them is up to you.

To the fullest extent the law allows, the author is not liable for any loss or harm arising from using or being unable to use the app, including loss of Medicaid coverage, missed deadlines, or lost records.

6. Privacy

  • No account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or Medicaid ID. Anything you type stays on your device.
  • We do not collect your records. There is no server that stores them. Backups and reports go only where you send them.
  • One anonymous count. The first time you open the app, it may send a single count that says only "the app was opened for the first time". It carries no name, ID or record. We use it to know roughly how many people use the app.
  • Web hosting. The app is delivered by a web host (Netlify). Like any website, the host's servers may briefly log technical details such as your IP address when your device downloads the app or its updates. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.
  • Links. Links to state websites take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting: in the app, go to Settings, then Delete everything. That erases your records and the app's files from your device. Then remove the icon from your home screen.
