West Virginia Medicaid

New work rules start in 2027.
Are you ready?

If you are on Medicaid and age 19 to 64, you may need to show that you work, volunteer or go to school. If you can't show it, you could lose your coverage.

Each month, you need ONE of these:

  • At least 80 hours of work, volunteer work, job training or school. You can add them together.
  • or
  • At least $580 earned from work.

The state checks its own records first. If you work for yourself, do gig work or get paid in cash, the state may have no record of your work, and it is up to you to prove it.
